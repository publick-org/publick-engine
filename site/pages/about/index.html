About

An independent site tracking public data about the City of {{ town.name }}. Not run by or affiliated with the city.

{% if site.get("network") %}

Part of {{ site.network }}

{{ site.name }} is one of the {{ site.network }} sites, which publish the public record of towns and cities every day. The name comes from Publick Occurrences Both Forreign and Domestick, the first newspaper in the American colonies. It was printed in Boston in 1690 and shut down after one issue for printing without permission.

AI summaries

Meeting summaries are written by AI ({{ config.summaries.model | model_name }}, by Anthropic). They aren't checked by a person before posting. The city's document is the official record, and every summary links to it.

Scanned agendas and minutes, which screen readers can't read, get a readable version of their full text, transcribed by AI and labeled that way. Where a document's own text can be laid out, the full text is the city's own words instead. Every page links the city's PDF.

Privacy

We count visits so we know which pages are useful. That's all.

  • No cookies, no ads, and no tracking across other sites.
  • What you search for, and which street you look up, is never recorded.
    • {% if wards_url %}
  • If you use "Find my ward," your location is checked on your device. It isn't sent anywhere or saved.
    • {% endif %}
  • Nothing that identifies you is kept, not even your IP address.

Counting is done by GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service. It notes the page, the site that linked here, and your screen size, along with the browser type and rough location (such as the country or state) that every website can see. We only look at the totals.{% if config.analytics.public_stats %} They're public.{% endif %}
