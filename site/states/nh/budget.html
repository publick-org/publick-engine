Property tax rate

The rate is in dollars for each $1,000 of a property's assessed value. It has four parts: the city's own, the county's, the statewide education tax, and the local schools'. New Hampshire names each rate by its tax year, which runs from April 1 to March 31; the DRA sets each town's rate in the fall.
